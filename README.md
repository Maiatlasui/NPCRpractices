# Character Detectives · 汉字侦探

A character-recognition game for beginning Chinese students, based on the conversations and vocabulary in Lessons 1–3 (updated 31 Oct 2026, more lessons to be added). Practise noticing character shapes and connecting complete words to their meanings.

## Included content

| Chapter | Distinct characters in that lesson | Questions |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Lesson 1 | 23 | 60 |
| Lesson 2 | 50 | 131 |
| Lesson 3 | 62 | 179 |
| Mixed revision | 98 across all lessons | 370 |

There are **98 distinct characters overall**, including characters in the dialogue names, and 17 lesson practice sets. Lesson character counts overlap. Every character has a dedicated shape question. Each word or expression also gets meaning recognition and reverse retrieval practice. Frequent words such as 也、都 and 喝 receive additional sentence questions.

The source is the supplied *Conversations Lessons 1–3* course slides. Slide headings and lesson numbers are excluded from the character inventory. The original presentation is not included in this package. Visible component notes are recognition cues. Words such as 咖啡 and 葡萄牙人 keep their complete-word meanings when students inspect individual characters. Dialogue names appear in separate sets.

## Start playing

Open the game link. Alternatively, if you have downloaded the game, double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.

## How to play

1. Read the question. Look closely at the character parts, their positions and any small differences between the choices.
2. Click or tap an answer. For keyboard play, use **Tab** to reach a control and **Enter** or **Space** to activate a button.
3. Read the feedback. It shows the correct answer, pinyin, meaning and a visual clue.
4. Select **Next clue** to continue.

Select **Need a shape clue?** for help before answering. Pinyin appears after your answer so you can concentrate on the writing first.

You will recognise characters from their parts, choose missing characters, match Chinese words to English meanings, find Chinese words for English meanings, and complete short expressions or sentences.

Learn complete words as you inspect their characters. For example, 咖啡 means “coffee”; noticing the shapes of 咖 and 啡 helps you recognise the complete word. Component descriptions are visual memory cues.

## Saving your place

Your place and scores save automatically when browser storage is available. To resume another day, use the **same browser and the same game address**. Different devices and browsers have separate progress records.

The game does not ask for your name, require an account or send your results to your teacher. See **Progress and privacy** below for details.

## Progress and privacy

Progress is stored in this browser's local storage for the current page address. It is not uploaded to a teacher account or shared with other students. The game requests no names or logins and contains no analytics or remote game services. GitHub Pages supplies the website hosting.

Use the same page address and browser to resume. Opening the downloaded file and opening the hosted site create separate progress records. Moving or renaming the page, clearing site data, private browsing or browser storage restrictions can prevent progress from being retained. The game remains playable when storage is unavailable. To remove all saved progress, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
